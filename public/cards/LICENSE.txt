Playing card faces in this folder

The 52 card images (rank + suit letter, e.g. KD.webp) are rendered from the
"Vector Playing Cards" deck:

  Original artwork: Byron Knoll, released into the public domain
  (https://code.google.com/archive/p/vector-playing-cards/)
  SVG collection used: https://github.com/notpeter/Vector-Playing-Cards
  (public domain, optionally WTFPL where the public domain is not recognised)

Rendered to 360 x 504 WebP on 16 Nov 2026. No changes to the artwork other
than the size and a 3.6 % change of proportion to the product's 5:7 card.
